About the salon

Where He & She both shine

One address in Toba Market where brides, families and gentlemen of Patran walk in for a cut and walk out feeling their absolute best.

He & She Beauty Hair Salon sits on Jakhal Road, Patran, and has grown into the town's trusted unisex salon — voted "Best Makeup Artist in Patran" on Google and rated a perfect 5.0★ from 929+ Justdial customers. From everyday haircuts to HD bridal transformations, the same careful hands handle every guest.

Colour services use L'Oréal Professionnel INOA — the professional ammonia-free colour system — and our salon is listed on L'Oréal's official salon locator. For thinning hair, we work alongside Indira Hair Weaving, Patiala — a hair-weaving name Punjab has trusted for 25 years — bringing specialist, non-surgical hair replacement within reach in Patran itself.
